Product learning loop

Listen to leaders

Observe real questions, confusing language and workarounds. Identify the decisions leaders are trying to make.

Release a useful slice

Ship one trusted question type with a clear evidence contract and measurable value.

Evaluate in the open

Track adoption, grounded-answer quality, abstention, latency, and leader feedback.

Keep, change, or remove

Improve features leaders rely on. Retire features that add complexity without value.

Decision memo

A one-page product recommendation grounded in the prototype's design principles.

Recommendation

Prioritise evidence-linked answers to the three most common workforce questions before expanding into open-ended AI recommendations.

Why

Reliability and habitual use matter more than the number of supported prompts. Build trust through clear sources, explicit limits and fast feedback.

Success measures

Weekly active leaders, repeat usage, answer usefulness, verified accuracy, abstention quality and time to useful answer.

Week 2 · Deliver

Measure repeat use, answer usefulness, unresolved questions, latency, and inappropriate outputs.

Week 4 · Decide
