06 / Transformation roadmap

Sequence outcomes, not just activities.

A decision-oriented roadmap connects discovery, validation, launch and scale to measurable business outcomes. These are suggested phases, not a committed delivery schedule.

NOW · Validate

Prove the customer problem

Interview stakeholders, establish baseline KPIs, test data availability and validate willingness to adopt.

Decision gate: Is the problem valuable and real?
NEXT · Pilot

Test the commercial impact

Run a bounded pilot with a defined customer segment, measurement plan and governance controls.

Decision gate: Is the outcome measurable?
LATER · Scale

Expand only with evidence

Scale the initiative when customer adoption, economic value and operational ownership meet agreed thresholds.

Decision gate: Is the case repeatable?

Transformation risks to surface

Data & compliance

Check GDPR obligations, lawful data use, security, interoperability and whether the intended AI use introduces additional regulatory duties.

Adoption & value capture

Identify process owners, workflow friction, change-management needs and a reliable baseline before claiming commercial benefits.
